About Drop The Strap Marketing

Named after the finisher. Built in Memphis. Google still matters. AI answers are a second game.

The Name

If you grew up watching Memphis wrestling, you know the moment. Jerry "The King" Lawler pulls down one strap of his singlet. The crowd erupts. The finisher is coming. Somebody's getting pinned.

That's the energy. When Drop The Strap shows up, the strap comes down and the work gets done. The name is a cultural reference point that makes the brand memorable, fun, and distinctly Memphis. The wrestling metaphors are seasoning — the substance is real search work delivered with personality.

The Rebuild

Last year's SEO playbook is incomplete. AI search engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews — are answering questions directly and naming businesses. That created a second game.

We look at both. Traditional SEO that still decides who shows up on Google, plus whether AI answers mention you when customers ask. Then you get a ranked list your team can run. We review. We don't take the keys.

How We Work

We're a small, focused team based in Memphis. Not an agency with a warehouse of clients and a cookie-cutter stack. We take on fights we can actually work.

Send the URL. We'll look around. If there's a fight worth taking, you'll hear from us. Nobody gets the CMS. Nobody gets a ranking promise. The look-around is how we decide whether to get in the ring.

Memphis Roots, National Reach

Drop The Strap Marketing is based in Memphis, Tennessee with two locations — Memphis and Collierville. We serve clients nationally because search work isn't locked to a zip code. But Memphis is home, and the wrestling culture that built this brand is Memphis to its core.
